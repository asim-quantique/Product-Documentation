## About This Guide

This guide explains how to use the Pickup and Drop app. It covers two experiences: the driver app and the customer tracking link. Use the relevant section based on your role.

### 1.1	Who Should Read This Guide

- Drivers who use the mobile app to manage and complete pickup and drop trips.
- Customers who use the tracking link to monitor vehicle delivery progress.

### 1.2	What You Need
- **Drivers:** An Android smartphone with internet access and the driver app installed from Google Play Store.
- **Customers:** A smartphone or computer with a web browser and access to the tracking link sent by email.

**Note: -** *This guide uses the Microsoft Writing Style Guide conventions. Steps describe actions and their results.*


## 2.	Feature Overview
The following table summarizes the key features available to each user role.

| Feature | Driver App | Customer Tracking Link |
|---|---|---|
| **Access** | Google Play Store – link received by email | Email link auto-sent when driver accepts trip |
| **Login Method** | Mobile number + OTP (sent to registered email) | No login required |
| **Trip View** | The assigned trip and all previously completed trips | Live tracking of current trip |
| **Status Updates** | Trip status throughout the journey | Receives each status change instantly |
| **Vehicle Evidence** | Photos (all angles) + walk-around video before pickup; drop-off photos at dealer | Views all uploaded evidence – photos, walk-around video, and drop-off photos – on the same tracking link |
| **Insurance** | Uploading pickup photos and video triggers certificate issuance; premium for that vehicle model deducted from dealer wallet | Not applicable |
| **Trip Completion** | System automatically marks trip complete once drop-off evidence is uploaded | Tracking page updates to Completed |
| **Driver Rating** | Not applicable | Rating popup on trip completion; can submit or simply ignore |
| **Transit Report** | Not applicable | Receives email with download link after trip completes |

## Part 1: Driver App
### 2.1	Driver App
The driver app lets you receive trip assignments, update trip status, capture vehicle evidence, and trigger insurance certification. All status updates appear in real time on the customer tracking link.

### 2.2	Install and Set Up the Driver App
Before your first trip, install the driver app on your Android device.

#### Install the Driver App

1)	Open the email you received with the driver app download link. The email contains a linkto download the driver app from Google Play Store.
2)	Select the download link in the email. Google Play Store opens on your device.
3)	Select Install. The app downloads and installs on your device.

### 2.3	Log In to the Driver App
Use your registered mobile number and a one-time password (OTP) to sign in.
Log In

1)	Open the driver app on your device.
2)	Enter your registered mobile number in the Mobile Number field.
3)	Select proceed. A one-time password is sent to your registered email address.
4)	Enter the OTP in the OTP field in the app.
5)	Select Verify. You are signed in. The app displays the assigned trip and all previously completed trips.

**Note: -** *The OTP is sent to your registered email address, not to your mobile number by SMS.*

### 2.4	View Assigned and Completed Trips
After signing in, the app home screen shows the assigned trip and a list of all previously completed trips.

**View Your Trips**

1)	On the home screen, review the assigned trip. The trip card shows key details such as the pickup address, drop-off address, and vehicle information.
2)	To view past trips, scroll to the Completed Trips section.
2.5	Accept or Reject a Trip
Open the assigned trip to review its details, then accept or decline it.
Accept or Reject a Trip
1)	Select the assigned trip from the home screen. The trip details page opens, showing the pickup address, drop-off address, vehicle details, and scheduled time.
2)	Review the trip details.
3)	To accept the trip, select Accept. The system confirms acceptance. A tracking link is automatically sent to the customer's registered email address, allowing the customer to follow the progress of their vehicle throughout the journey.
4)	To decline the trip, select Reject. The trip is removed from your list.

**Note: -** *As soon as you accept a trip, the customer receives an email with a live tracking link and can follow the progress of their vehicle throughout the journey.*

### 2.6	Update Trip Status

Update the trip status at each stage of the journey. Every status change immediately reflects on the customer's tracking page.

#### Set Status to On the Way to Customer

1)	On the trip details page, select On the Way to Customer. The status updates. The customer's tracking link immediately shows that the driver is on the way.

#### Mark Arrived at Customer Location

2)	When you arrive at the customer's address, select Arrived at Customer Location. The app confirms your arrival and unlocks the pickup evidence process.

### 2.7	Capture Vehicle Photos and Video at Pickup
After marking your arrival, you must document the vehicle's condition before the pickup begins. You are required to capture photos from all angles and record a separate walk-around video. The app will not allow you to proceed until both are uploaded.

#### Capture Pickup Evidence
1)	On the trip page, select Capture Vehicle Photos. The camera opens.
2)	Photograph the vehicle from all angles: front, rear, driver side, and passenger side. Each photo is added to the upload queue.

#### Record the Walk-Around Video
1)	Select Record Walk-Around Video. The camera switches to video mode.
2)	Walk slowly around the entire vehicle and record its condition from all sides.
3)	Select Stop Recording when the walk-around is complete. The video is added to the upload queue alongside the photos.
Upload Pickup Evidence
1)	Select Upload. The photos and walk-around video are uploaded to the system.


---

**Important:** *Once the photos and video are uploaded, the system automatically issues an insurance certificate. The insurance premium for that vehicle model is then automatically deducted from the dealer’s wallet. You cannot proceed to the next step until the upload is successful.*

---


**Note: -** *After a successful upload, the customer can view the uploaded vehicle photos through the tracking link.*

### 2.8	Update Status: On the Way to Dealer
After completing the pickup documentation, update your status so the customer knows the vehicle is in transit.

#### Set Status to On the Way to Dealer
1)	Select On the Way to Dealer. The status updates. The customer's tracking page immediately reflects that the vehicle is in transit to the dealer.

### 2.9	Complete the Drop-Off at the Dealer
After reaching the dealer, capture drop-off photos as proof of delivery. Once the required evidence is successfully uploaded, the system automatically marks the trip as completed.

#### Capture Drop-Off Photos and Complete the Trip

1)	When you reach the dealer location, select Capture Drop-Off Photos. The camera opens.
2)	Photograph the vehicle to confirm its delivery condition. The photos serve as proof of delivery and are added to the upload queue.
3)	Select Upload. The drop-off photos are uploaded. Once all required evidence is successfully uploaded, the system automatically marks the trip as Completed.

---

**Important:** *The trip is marked as completed automatically by the system once the drop-off evidence is successfully uploaded. There is no separate button to complete the trip.*

---

**Note: -** *After trip completion, the customer receives a driver rating popup on their tracking page and a separate email with a link to download the transit report.*

### Part 2: Customer Tracking Link

### 2.1	Customer Tracking Link
As a customer, you do not need to install any app. When the driver accepts your trip, a unique tracking link is automatically sent to your registered email address. You can open this link in any web browser on your phone or computer. The same link remains active throughout the journey and shows all trip updates and evidence in one place.

### 2.2	Open Your Tracking Link
Access the Tracking Page
1)	Open the email you received from Pickup and Drop. The email contains a unique tracking link for your trip.
2)	Select the tracking link. Your default browser opens the live tracking page.
**Note: -** *The tracking link is unique to your trip and requires no sign-in. Keep this link you will use it throughout the journey and to view all final evidence.*

### 2.3	Follow Real-Time Trip Status
The tracking page updates automatically each time the driver changes trip status. You do not need to refresh the page.

### Trip Status Stages
The following statuses appear on your tracking page during a trip, in order:
- **Trip Accepted:** The driver has accepted your trip. This is when your tracking link is sent.
- **On the Way to Customer:** The driver is travelling to your location.
- **Arrived at Customer Location:** The driver has reached your address and is beginning the pickup process.
- **On the Way to Dealer:** Your vehicle is in transit to the dealer.
- **Trip Completed:** The vehicle has been delivered to the dealer.

### 2.4	View Vehicle Photos
After the driver uploads pickup photos at your location, you can review them directly on the tracking page.

#### View Uploaded Vehicle Photos
1)	On the tracking page, scroll to the Vehicle Photos section. Thumbnails of all uploaded photos appear.
2)	Select any thumbnail to view the full-size photo.

**Note: -** *Photos are available as soon as the driver uploads them. Refresh the page if photos do not appear immediately.*

### 2.5	View All Trip Evidence
After the trip is completed, you can view all evidence uploaded by the driver pickup photos, the walk-around video, and drop-off photos on the same tracking link you have been using throughout the journey. No new link is sent for this.

#### View Pickup and Drop-Off Photos
1)	On the same tracking link, scroll to the Trip Evidence section. The pickup photos, walk-around video, and drop-off photos uploaded by the driver are all displayed.
2)	Select any photo or the video to view it in full size.

**Note: -** *All evidence pickup photos, walk-around video, and drop-off photos is available on the same tracking link you received when the driver accepted the trip. You do not receive a new link for this.*

### 2.6	Rate Your Driver
When the trip is marked as completed, a driver rating popup appears on your tracking page.

#### Submit a Driver Rating
1)	When the rating popup appears, you have two options:
2)	To submit a rating, select your preferred star rating and then select Submit. Your rating is recorded.
3)	To skip the rating, simply ignore the popup. No action is needed. You can continue to view the completed trip details.

**Note: -** *Rating the driver is optional. The popup does not block you from viewing the rest of the tracking page.*

### 2.7	Download the Transit Report
After the trip is completed, Pickup and Drop sends you a separate email containing a link to download the transit report for your records.

#### Download Your Transit Report
1)	Open the trip completion email from Pickup and Drop. The email contains a link to download the transit report.
2)	Select the download link. The report opens or downloads, depending on your browser settings.
3)	Save the file to your device for your records.

## 3.	Troubleshooting

### 3.1	Driver App Issues
**I did not receive the app download email.** Check all folders in your email inbox, including spam and junk. If the email is not there, contact the Pickup and Drop service team to have the download link resent.

**The app is not showing my assigned trip.** Ensure you have a stable internet connection and refresh the home screen. If the issue persists, sign out and sign back in.

**Photos or the walk-around video are not uploading.** Check your mobile data or Wi-Fi connection. Ensure photos are in focus and not corrupted. Re-capture and try uploading again.

**The trip is not being marked as completed.** The system marks the trip as completed automatically once all required drop-off evidence is successfully uploaded. Verify that the upload finished without errors and try again if needed.

### 3.2	Customer Tracking Link Issues

**I did not receive the tracking link.** The link is sent when the driver accepts the trip. Check your spam or junk folder. If not found, contact the Pickup and Drop service team to have the link resent.

**The tracking page is not updating.** The page updates automatically. A manual browser refresh may help if your connection is intermittent.

**Vehicle photos are not visible on the tracking page.** Photos appear after the driver uploads them at the pickup location. If the driver status shows Arrived at Customer Location or later, try refreshing the page.

**I cannot see the walk-around video or drop-off photos.** All evidence including the walk-around video and drop-off photos becomes visible on the same tracking link once the trip is marked as completed. Refresh the page if items do not appear immediately.

**I did not receive the transit report email.** Check your spam folder. Allow a few minutes after trip completion for the email to arrive. Contact support if it does not appear.
